# NetPlex demo assets

`public/assets/hero-dark-fantasy.png` er et originalt demobillede genereret med det indbyggede ImageGen-værktøj. Det er ikke hentet fra Plex og illustrerer ingen virkelig film. I et tilsluttet bibliotek erstattes det af den valgte films Plex-baggrund.

Genereringsprompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic background illustration for the hero of NetPlex, a locally running personal movie streaming app. Generate an ORIGINAL high-end dark fantasy film still, landscape 16:9, 2048x1152 if available. A solitary hooded traveler in a weathered black cloak, seen from behind, stands on a rocky mountain overlook on the RIGHT THIRD of the image. Vast mist-filled valleys, distant cold mountains, winding pale river far below, soft overcast light. Rich believable cloak folds, subtle desaturated slate blues and charcoal, atmospheric depth, realistic concept art with restrained cinematic grain. LEFT HALF should consist of quiet dark misty landscape and low contrast negative space suitable for overlaying a very large pale title, but DO NOT add any lettering. Mood: mysterious, beautiful, contemplative, polished movie cinematography. No text, no logos, no UI, no frame, no watermark. This is a background illustration, not a website screenshot.

De øvrige fiktive demofilm har originale, kodebaserede plakater i `public/demo.js` og `public/styles.css`.

`public/assets/unbounded-latin.woff2` er Unbounded 5.3.0 fra `@fontsource-variable/unbounded`; licensen er gemt som `public/assets/FONT-LICENSE.txt`.
